## 建筑巨构（8）

**01 · 沙漠中的巨型水坝** · 16:9 · raw · Large
> A monolithic dam wall of banded concrete sealing an entire canyon, its face divided into a grid of identical spillway arches, staircases switchbacking up the left abutment. Dry rust streaks run from every joint. Below, a sliver of river and a cluster of tin-roof houses no bigger than dice. A single truck on the crest road is a moving speck. Hard midday sun flattens the facade while the canyon floor stays in deep shadow; heat shimmer rises off the concrete. Shot on a 50mm lens from the opposite rim at eye level, the wall spanning the full width of the frame. Architectural visualization with documentary photography realism, desaturated ochre and grey, subtle grain.

Dawn light strikes only the top third, the rest in cool blue-grey haze. A flock of birds crosses near the base, invisible at this distance except as faint specks. Shot on a 16mm ultra-wide from far below, vertical composition, the spire extends beyond the top edge. Romantic oil painting, sublime scale, warm gold against cold violet.
