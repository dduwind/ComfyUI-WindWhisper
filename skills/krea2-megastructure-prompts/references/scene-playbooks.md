# 巨构场景剧本（A–E）

五类剧本。每类给出：**典型主体 → 结构语法 → 材质词 → 环境词 → 镜头建议 → 尺度锚定套路 → 完整范例**。
按主体类型挑一个（或跨类组合，跨类往往更有意思：例如「地质巨构 + 建筑巨构」= 从山体里长出来的城市）。

---

## A · 建筑巨构 Architectural Megastructure

**典型主体**：巨塔、巨型拱门、悬空要塞、层叠立体城市、巨型水坝、巨型教堂/神殿、巨型阶梯、地下深井城市、巨型城墙、巨型桥墩、空心巨像内部、巨型穹顶。

**结构语法**（挑 1–2 个，别全堆）
`cantilevered terraces` 悬挑台地 · `stacked modular habitation blocks` 层叠模块居住体 · `corbelled masonry vaults` 叠涩拱顶 · `buttressed spine` 扶壁脊 · `lattice of exposed trusses` 外露桁架格构 · `inverted ziggurat` 倒阶梯金字塔 · `suspended catenary shells` 悬链壳 · `arcology grid` 生态建筑网格 · `flying buttresses` 飞扶壁 · `hanging gardens on every ledge` 每层挑台挂满花园。

**材质**：weathered board-formed concrete 清水混凝土 · oxidized copper cladding 氧化铜板 · sandstone blocks with erosion-rounded edges 边缘被侵蚀磨圆砂岩块 · rusted corten steel 耐候钢 · marble veined with lichen 长地衣的云石 · glazed ceramic tile in faded cobalt 褪色的钴蓝釉面砖 · bronze ribs green with verdigris 生铜绿的青铜肋。

**环境**：sea of cloud 云海 · dust haze 尘霾 · monsoon rain curtains 季风雨幕 · overgrown with vines and small trees 长满藤蔓小树 · terraced rice fields on upper decks 上层台地的水稻田 · fishing boats moored to the base 泊在基座下的渔船。

**镜头**：极低角度仰视（14–24mm）· 高空俯视看整体几何 · 穿过拱门的框景 · 沿一面墙的局部特写（只拍窗与接缝，靠极小的人影暗示尺度）。

**尺度锚定套路**
- 人：`a lone figure at the base is no taller than a fingernail on the structure`
- 城市：`a single window opening is large enough to swallow a city block`
- 云：`clouds drift at mid-height, swallowing the lower third`
- 数字：`the tower rises over four kilometers, its summit lost in cloud`

**范例 A1 · 悬空要塞（垂直，9:16）**
> A colossal tiered sky fortress clinging to a sheer cliff, inverted ziggurat of weathered board-formed concrete and oxidized copper, each cantilevered terrace crowded with terraced rice fields, shrines and tiny timber houses, waterfalls spilling from the uppermost deck into cloud. Clouds drift at mid-height and swallow the lower third entirely; only the rusted undersides and the hanging root systems are visible below. Late afternoon sun rakes across the terraces from the left, throwing long hard shadows and catching the mist in volumetric shafts. A single fishing boat far below is the size of a fingernail. Shot on a 16mm wide-angle lens from an extremely low angle looking up, the structure fills the frame and its summit extends beyond the top edge. Cinematic matte painting concept art, epic scale, atmospheric perspective, muted jade and amber palette, fine painterly detail.

**范例 A2 · 巨塔内部（竖井，9:16）**
> Interior of a colossal hollow tower, a vertical city built into the inner wall — thousands of stacked modular dwellings, catwalks and shrines clinging to a lattice of exposed iron trusses, receding upward into a haze-lit void. A distant skylight far above throws a single column of dusty volumetric light down the shaft. Everything below the tenth ring dissolves into blue-grey atmosphere. Tiny figures on the catwalks are specks. Shot with a 24mm tilt-shift lens from a precarious catwalk, looking both up and down, extreme vertical composition. Grim industrial fantasy concept art, dense structural detail, high dynamic range, cool blue-grey with a single warm light shaft.

## B · 生物巨构 Creature / Leviathan Megastructure

**典型主体**：巨兽、巨龙、 Leviathan 海兽、巨型神像、巨兽骨架、寄生结构体、半生物半建筑的活体、巨虫、巨型植物-生物混合体。

**结构语法**
`segmented plating like armor` 分节甲板 · `exposed ribcage forming an inhabited arcology` 肋骨成为可居住体 · `vertebrate spine supporting a city on its back` 脊柱驮着一座城 · `tendrils anchoring into the ground like suspension cables` 触须如悬索锚入地面 · `carapace terraced into settlements` 甲壳被开垦成聚落 · `gills that vent steam` 排蒸汽的鳃 · `bioluminescent veins pulsing under translucent skin` 半透皮下发光血管。

**材质**：petrified hide 石化皮 · coral-encrusted shell 长满珊瑚的外壳 · chitinous plates 几丁质甲片 · mossy bone 长苔的骨 · translucent membrane with glowing veins 半透膜与发光血管 · matted fur with wind-carved patterns 被风吹出纹路的毛。

**环境**：dust storm 沙暴 · ocean swell 涌浪 · volcanic ash 火山灰 · migrating bird flocks 鸟群 · parasites and barnacles 寄生与藤壶 · scavenger settlements on its back 背上的拾荒聚落。

**镜头**：局部特写（只拍一只眼睛/一段甲片，靠纹理细节显大）· 极低角度仰视看腹部 · 远景全身 + 极小人影 · 从背上看聚落。

**尺度锚定套路**
- `a village of dozens of houses sits between two ribs`
- `birds flock around its shoulders like gnats`
- `ships sail beneath its belly without it noticing`
- 局部：只写纹理与附着生态，让"大"由"这是一小块皮肤"暗示

**范例 B1 · 驮城巨兽（横幅，2.35:1）**
> An immense petrified leviathan striding across a salt flat, its carapace terraced into a settlement of dozens of tiny mud-brick houses, prayer flags and ladders strung between the segmented armor plates. Corals and barnacles encrust its lower flanks; mats of dried algae hang from the jaw. It moves slowly, tendrils like suspension cables anchoring into the ground with each step, raising curtains of pale dust. A caravan of six camels crosses beneath its belly, ant-sized. Low sun behind the creature produces a blazing rim light and long atmospheric haze; the far legs fade into golden dust. Shot on a 35mm lens from a low dune at extreme distance, the beast spanning nearly the entire frame. Epic fantasy matte painting, monumental scale, warm ochre and bone palette, volumetric dust, painterly realism.

**范例 B2 · 巨兽皮肤特写（局部，1:1）**
> Extreme macro of a colossal creature's hide — petrified plates the size of garage doors, each one bordered by deep fissures where mosses, ferns and tiny nesting birds have taken hold. Translucent seams between the plates pulse with faint bioluminescent blue. Fine dust has settled in every crevice. Shallow depth of field, only the nearest plate edge in focus while the rest falls away into soft bokeh, raking low sunlight across the rough mineral texture. Macro photography look with a 100mm lens, hyper-detailed surface study, muted grey-green and amber, no visible horizon to give scale away.

## C · 自然 / 地质巨构 Geological Megastructure

**典型主体**：巨型峡谷、巨树、巨浪、冰川、火山口、巨型晶洞、云墙、沙丘、天坑、巨瀑、石林、海底深渊、巨型拱岩。

**结构语法**
`stratified rock bands` 层理岩带 · `columnar basalt joints` 玄武岩柱状节理 · `undercut overhang` 底部掏空的悬挑 · `glacial calving face` 冰川崩解面 · `dendritic root fan` 根系扇形展开 · `wave curling into a hollow tube` 卷成管状的浪。

**材质**：banded sandstone 带状砂岩 · glacial blue ice with crevasse 冰裂缝的冰川蓝冰 · wet basalt 湿玄武岩 · wind-polished limestone 风磨石灰岩 · crystalline quartz faces 石英晶面。

**环境**：mist layered between strata 层间雾 · snowmelt runoff 融雪径流 · seabird colonies 海鸟群落 · stunted trees clinging to ledges 矮树 · aurora / storm front 极光/风暴锋面。

**镜头**：远景全景 + 极小参照 · 沿崖壁的高空平视 · 谷底的极端仰视 · 巨浪的管内视角。

**尺度锚定套路**
- `a kayak is a white speck at the base of the wave`
- `the waterfall's spray forms its own weather system`
- `clouds form at the level of the lower branches`

**范例 C1 · 巨浪（横幅，2.35:1）**
> A single wave of impossible scale curling into a hollow tube, glacial blue-green and translucent at the lip where morning light passes through, the inner face a smooth barrel of deep teal glass. Spray shears off the crest into its own weather, forming a low rainbow. A lone kayak is a white speck at the base, impossibly small. Shot from inside the barrel with a 16mm fisheye, the arch of water filling the entire frame, sunlight blasting through the translucent lip ahead. Photorealistic action photography, 1/2000s frozen detail, high contrast, salt spray texture, cool cyan against warm sun.

**范例 C2 · 世界巨树（垂直，9:16）**
> A tree of geological scale rising out of a cloud sea, its trunk a fluted column of banded stone-like bark, buttress roots spreading wider than a city district and vanishing into white. Entire ecosystems occupy the lower branches — hanging moss, ferns, waterfalls and small wooden platforms. Clouds form at the level of the lower branches and hide the base entirely. Single shaft of dawn light rakes across the trunk. Shot on a 24mm wide-angle from a neighbouring ridge at eye level with the canopy floor, the trunk occupying the right third. Romantic landscape painting, Hudson River School influence, sublime scale, deep greens and golden light.

## D · 机械 / 载具巨构 Mechanical & Vehicle Megastructure

**典型主体**：星舰、巨型机甲、太空电梯、轨道环、巨型采矿机、航母、钻井平台、飞艇、巨型挖掘机、太空站、行星发动机。

**结构语法**
`greeble-dense hull plating` 密集机械细节的舰体板 · `exposed gantry cranes` 外露龙门吊 · `modular cargo spine` 模块化货舱脊 · `radiator fins` 散热鳍 · `tension cables and counterweights` 张索与配重 · `walkways and service scaffolding at absurd scale` 荒谬尺度的检修栈桥。

**材质**：pitted hull steel 麻点舰钢 · heat-scored tiles 烧灼隔热瓦 · cryogenic frost 低温霜 · warning-striped hazard paint 警示条纹漆 · hydraulic pistons slick with oil 油亮的液压杆。

**环境**：launch plume 发射羽流 · drydock scaffolding 船坞脚手架 · fog bank 雾堤 · orbital starfield 轨道星场 · dust plume from tracks 履带扬尘。

**镜头**：干船坞仰视 · 沿舰体的长焦平视压缩 · 机甲脚部仰视 + 人影 · 轨道环的极端远景。

**尺度锚定套路**
- `service walkways crowded with workers the size of ants`
- `a city skyline is visible at the base of the landing leg`
- 数字：`the hull stretches eleven kilometers bow to stern`

**范例 D1 · 干船坞中的星舰（垂直，9:16）**
> A colossal interstellar haulier standing vertical in a drydock, eleven kilometers bow to stern, its hull a greeble-dense landscape of pitted steel plates, radiator fins and modular cargo spines. Service scaffolding wraps it in layers; walkways crowded with workers the size of ants crawl across the mid-section. Cryogenic frost feathers off the lower tanks while steam vents in slow white columns. Cold pre-dawn light from the left, warm sodium dock lights from below, deep shadow between the fins. Shot on a 14mm ultra-wide from the dock floor looking straight up, the hull receding into haze above. Hard science-fiction concept art, gritty industrial realism, desaturated steel blue with sodium orange accents, extreme scale.

**范例 D2 · 机甲足部仰视（垂直，2:3）**
> Extreme low-angle close-up of a battle mech's foot planted in a flooded street, hydraulic pistons slick with oil and mud, hazard-striped armor plating pitted with shrapnel, water sheeting off the treads. Above, the leg rises out of frame into smoke. A handful of fleeing figures near a doorway are no taller than the ankle joint. Sodium firelight from the right, backlit smoke, shallow depth of field on the nearest piston. 35mm lens, low angle, photorealistic cinematic still, anamorphic flare, teal and orange grade, film grain.

## E · 超现实 / 混合巨构 Surreal & Hybrid

**典型主体**：悬浮岛、倒悬山、世界树、天空裂缝、巨型几何体悬浮于海面、云上的门、巨型镜面、不可能结构（彭罗斯楼梯尺度化）、星体残骸。

**结构语法**
`impossible geometry` 不可能几何 · `floating monolith` 悬浮巨石 · `inverted mountain with a city on its underside` 底面建城的倒悬山 · `a single door standing in the sea` 立在海中的门 · `orbital ring seen from the ground` 地面看到的轨道环 · `a crack in the sky leaking light` 天空裂缝漏光。

**材质**：polished obsidian 抛光黑曜石 · mirror-finish chrome 镜面铬 · petrified cloud 石化云 · marble fused with circuitry 与电路融合的云石 · translucent crystal 半透晶体。

**情绪**：敬畏、诡异、静谧、神性。超现实巨构最忌喧闹——**减少元素，加大负空间**。

**镜头**：极简对称构图 · 巨大负空间 · 极小人影置于画面下三分之一 · 超长宽比。

**范例 E1 · 海中之门（横幅，2.35:1）**
> A single monumental doorframe of weathered black stone standing alone in a flat, mirror-still sea under a blank pearl sky. No walls, no structure — only the frame, thirty stories tall, its lintel carved with eroded glyphs and hung with long strands of kelp. A thin waterfall of light falls through the opening. One tiny rowboat approaches from the right. Perfect reflection in the water, vast negative space, horizon placed low. Shot on a 50mm lens from water level. Surrealist fine-art photograph, minimal palette of slate grey and bone white, soft even light, meditative, impossible scale.

**范例 E2 · 倒悬山（垂直，9:16）**
> An inverted mountain range hanging from the sky, its peaks pointing down toward a still lake, a terraced monastery clinging to the underside of the highest cliff with prayer flags and a single waterfall that falls upward into mist. The lake below holds a perfect inverted reflection. Two tiny boats drift on the water, dwarfed. Golden dusk light rakes across the rock from below, warm against cold violet sky. Shot on a 24mm wide-angle, vertical composition, the mountain filling the upper two thirds. Surreal fantasy matte painting, dreamlike atmosphere, soft volumetric haze, deep violet and amber palette.
