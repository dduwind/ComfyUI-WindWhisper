## 四、打光方案模板

### 方案 A：单窗光（最经典迪金斯）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Deakins Single Window:

• KEY: Large soft window light (camera-left or camera-right), diffused through curtain/frost
• FILL: None — or minimal bounce from opposite wall (ratio 4:1 to 8:1)
• PRACTICAL: One table lamp providing warm accent (visible in frame)
• AMBIENT: Low — room naturally dark except window area
• SHADOW: Deep, graduated, detail preserved in darkest areas
• COLOR TEMP: Window = cool daylight (5600K), Practical = warm tungsten (2700K)
• MOOD: Intimate, contemplative, truthful
```

### 方案 B：单灯泡旅馆（《老无所依》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Deakins Motel Room:

• KEY: Single overhead practical bulb (visible in frame), warm tungsten
• FILL: None
• AMBIENT: Extremely low — darkness surrounds pool of light
• SHADOW: Deep, surrounding, character moves in and out of light
• COLOR TEMP: Warm tungsten (2700K) surrounded by cool darkness
• MOOD: Lonely, menacing, fate approaching in the dark
```

### 方案 C：霓虹剪影（《大破天幕杀机》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Deakins Neon Silhouette:

• KEY: Large neon or LED colored backlight behind glass/window
• FILL: None — subjects entirely in silhouette
• PRACTICAL: Neon signs, city lights visible through glass
• AMBIENT: Dark — only backlight
• SHADOW: Subjects are the shadow — complete silhouette
• COLOR TEMP: Cool blue/cyan neon (8000K+)
• MOOD: Elegant danger, mythic confrontation
```

### 方案 D：反乌托邦单色区（《银翼杀手2049》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Deakins Monochromatic Zone:

• KEY: Single dominant color source washing entire environment
• FILL: Same color at lower intensity
• AMBIENT: Color-saturated atmospheric haze/fog
• SHADOW: Tinted with same color, not true black
• COLOR TEMP: Varies by zone —
  - Orange zone: 2200K (apocalyptic warm)
  - Blue zone: 9000K (cold dystopian)
  - White zone: 6500K (sterile clinical)
• MOOD: Otherworldly, isolated, color as emotion
```

### 方案 E：自然天光战场（《1917》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Deakins Natural Overcast:

• KEY: Natural overcast sky — soft omnidirectional light
• FILL: Natural — from sky and ground bounce
• PRACTICAL: None in exterior; flares/explosions for night
• AMBIENT: High ambient ratio, minimal contrast
• SHADOW: Soft, undirectional, mud and earth absorbing light
• COLOR TEMP: Cool overcast daylight (6500-7500K)
• MOOD: Relentless, exposed, nowhere to hide
```
